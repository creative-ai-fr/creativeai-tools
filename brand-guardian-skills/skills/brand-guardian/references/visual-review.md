# Visual review

Use only the supplied asset and accessible profile evidence. Describe observable properties before interpreting them.

## Check relevant dimensions

Depending on the profile, inspect logo/version/clear space, color, typography, layout, image style, product depiction, claims/text visible in the image, and channel-specific requirements. Do not run a generic checklist as if every dimension were a brand rule.

## Evidence discipline

- Cite the profile item and the profile's original source locator, not just “the guidelines say.”
- For asset evidence, point to a visible region or quote visible copy. Do not invent a page number for the asset.
- Separate observation (“logo appears close to the edge”) from conclusion (“violates the 24 px margin rule”). The conclusion needs a readable explicit rule and enough image resolution to measure reliably.
- If scale, crop, background, or resolution makes a measurement uncertain, report `UNKNOWN` for that check and explain what source file or measurement would resolve it.
- A new composition, unusual palette, or departure from campaign examples is not itself a violation. Use `OBSERVATION` for novelty when useful.

## Output

Keep image findings localized: region/component, observed property, applicable evidence, outcome, and smallest adjustment. Do not recommend changing unrelated composition, subject, lighting, or art direction.
